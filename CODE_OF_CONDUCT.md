# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Contributors are expected to keep discussions respectful, specific and focused
on the work. Constructive feedback, clear technical reasoning and patience with
different levels of experience are welcome.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information
or sharing credentials and private evidence from this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that violate these standards, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and made in
good faith to protect the project and its participants.
